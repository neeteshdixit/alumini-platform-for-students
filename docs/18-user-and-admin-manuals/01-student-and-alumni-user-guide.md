## 1. Welcome to AlumniConnect

**AlumniConnect** is your institution's official professional networking, mentorship, and career acceleration platform. Designed exclusively for your college community, the platform connects you with verified alumni and students from your own university, enabling authentic career guidance, mentorship, internship referrals, and meaningful professional relationships.

---

## 2. Getting Started: Registration & Onboarding

### 2.1 Creating Your Account
1. Open your browser and navigate to `https://alumniconnect.edu/signup`.
2. **Select Your Institution:**
   - Step 1: Select your **State** (e.g. *Madhya Pradesh*).
   - Step 2: Select your **District** (e.g. *Bhopal*).
   - Step 3: Select your **College** from the filtered dropdown.
   - *Tip:* If your college is not listed, click *"Can't find your college? Request to add it"* to submit your institution for fast administrative approval.
3. **Fill in Your Credentials:** Enter your Full Name, Email Address, and choose a strong password ($\ge 8$ characters with numbers and symbols).
4. **Email OTP Verification:**
   - Check your inbox for a 6-digit verification code.
   - Enter the code in the modal within 10 minutes to verify your account.

## 3. Building a Standout Profile

Your profile is your digital business card on campus. A complete profile significantly boosts your visibility and recommendation score:
1. **Profile Picture & Bio:** Upload a professional headshot and write a concise 2–3 sentence bio summarizing your academic passions and career trajectory.
2. **Domain & Skills:** Select your domain (`Tech`, `Non-Tech`, or `Both`) and add verified skill tags (e.g. `React`, `Python`, `Financial Modeling`).
3. **Experience & Projects:** Document past internships, club leadership roles, hackathon wins, and technical projects.
4. **Certificates & Achievements:** Upload PDF certificates to display verifiable inline previews on your profile.

## 4. Discovering Alumni & Same-College Networking

### 4.1 Browsing Your Alma Mater Directory
- Click **"Directory"** in the top navigation bar.
- The directory automatically displays verified alumni from **your exact college**.
- Use filters to find seniors by:
  - **Department:** Filter by your branch (e.g. *Computer Science*, *Mechanical*, *Commerce*).
  - **Graduation Batch:** Find recent graduates (1–2 years out) or senior leaders (5+ years out).
  - **Skills & Domain:** Filter by specific programming languages or industries.

### 4.2 Connecting & The 2-Minute Introductory Chat
- **Sending a Connection Request:** Click `Connect` on any alumnus card. Once they accept, unlimited messaging and video calls unlock.
- **The 2-Minute Warm Chat:**
  - Need urgent advice before connecting? Click `Message` to start an introductory chat.
  - You have **2 minutes of free messaging** from the first message sent.
  - Use this window to introduce yourself politely: *"Hi Ananya! I'm a 3rd-year CS student at your alma mater. I saw your work at Google and would love to connect regarding internship prep."*
  - To continue chatting after 2 minutes, send a connection request!

## 5. Booking Mentorship Sessions

1. Navigate to **"Mentorship"** in the top bar.
2. Browse alumni marked as *"Open to Mentorship"*. Check their available capacity slots (e.g. *"2 of 3 slots filled"*).
3. Click `Book Mentorship`.
4. Enter your concrete session goals (e.g. *Resume review, mock interview*).
5. Once accepted, your shared Mentorship Workspace unlocks:
   - Check off goals as you complete them.
   - Add shared session notes.
   - Launch high-definition 1-on-1 video calls with zero third-party software required.
6. Submit your completion rating to help your mentor climb the **Alumni Leaderboard**!

---

## 6. Jobs, Internships & Alumni Referrals

1. Navigate to **"Opportunities"**.
2. Opportunities display a personalized **Skill Match Percentage** based on your profile skills.
3. Look for roles with the green badge: *"Referral Available"*.
4. Click `Request Referral`, submit a polite cover note, and attach your uploaded PDF resume.
5. Track your referral progress directly in your dashboard (`Applied` -> `Shortlisted` -> `Referred`).

---

## 7. Safety, Reporting & Support

AlumniConnect strictly enforces a zero-tolerance policy for harassment, spam, and unprofessional conduct:
- **Blocking a User:** In any chat or profile menu, click `More Options` -> `Block User`. This immediately cuts all communication.
- **Reporting Violations:** Click `Report User`, select the category (*Harassment*, *Spam*, *Impersonation*), and provide details. Reports are triaged 24/7 by our moderation team.
- **Support:** Need assistance? Reach out to your college placement cell or email `support@alumniconnect.edu`.
